# CV Site

Personal portfolio site for Jack Liu, aimed at recruiters and hiring managers for senior finance roles.

## Language

**Positioning**:
The single claim the site makes about Jack: an FP&A leader whose edge is building the analytics (models, pipelines, ML forecasts, dashboards) behind the numbers. Finance comes first and technology is the differentiator.
_Avoid_: "analytics leader", "data leader" as the headline identity

**Proof point**:
A single quantified accomplishment from Jack's career (e.g. forecast miss cut, effort saved). The site's claims rest on these. A published proof point about the current employer's internal performance or strategy is stated as a percentage or in general terms, never in dollars. Public scale figures (e.g. the $4B portfolio) may appear as-is.
_Avoid_: stat, metric (when meaning a career accomplishment)

**Signature scene**:
A full-screen, scroll-driven moment that turns one **Proof point** into an animated chart built from Jack's own numbers. Signature scenes are the site's visual centrepieces and stand in for decorative imagery. Labelled "Case study" on the site.
_Avoid_: hero image, illustration

**The machine**:
Jack's way of working, shown as a four-stage pipeline: Data → Model → Forecast → Decision. Each tool Jack uses belongs to one stage. It connects the **Positioning** to his technical skills. Labelled "Approach" on the site.
_Avoid_: tech stack, skills list

**Capability**:
One of four areas Jack offers an employer: FP&A, Business Partnering, Leadership, AI/ML & Automation.
_Avoid_: skill (too granular), competency

**Public resume**:
The one resume downloadable from the site: a scrubbed master version that follows the same disclosure rules as the site. It's distinct from the **Tailored resumes** Jack sends privately for specific applications.
_Avoid_: CV (ambiguous with the site itself)

**Tailored resume**:
A private, per-application resume (.docx) kept outside the site. It is never published.

**Off the clock**:
The small personal section (Muay Thai, latte art). It humanizes the page without competing with the **Positioning**.
Labelled "Interests" on the site.
_Avoid_: hobbies (as an internal term)

**Recruiter**:
The primary reader: a recruiter or hiring manager for Director-level FP&A, finance transformation, or planning & analytics roles, who scans the page in seconds.
